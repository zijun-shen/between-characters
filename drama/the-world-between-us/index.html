About the Drama
劇集介紹
《我們與惡的距離》是一部探討隨機殺人事件後社會各方反應的台灣影集，涉及媒體倫理、司法制度、受害者家屬與加害者家庭等議題。
The World Between Us is an award-winning Taiwanese drama that explores the aftermath of a mass shooting and the complex questions of justice, media ethics, and social responsibility.
