電影裡的語言與文化

01

也太……了吧！？

yě tài… le ba That’s so…! / That’s way too…!

「你也太神了吧？」

「你男朋友也太樂觀了吧，這麼快就走出來囉？」

Usage

「也太……了吧」用來強調說話者的情緒與評價，語氣比「太……了吧」更強烈、更誇張。中間可以搭配正面或負面的詞。

“Yě tài… le ba” emphasizes the speaker’s emotion and evaluation. It is stronger and more expressive than “tài… le ba” and can be used with both positive and negative words.

Examples

才學中文三個月就考過 TOCFL A2，也太厲害了吧！

You’ve only been studying Chinese for three months and already passed TOCFL A2? That’s amazing!

這杯飲料也太甜了吧！誰喝得完啊？

This drink is way too sweet. Who could possibly finish it?

你遲到一個小時，也太誇張了吧！

You’re an hour late? That’s ridiculous!

02

拜託，我誰啊？

bài tuō, wǒ shéi a? Come on—you know who I am!
A

你也太神了吧？

B

拜託，我誰啊？

「拜託，我誰？我要辦大案子的耶。」

Usage

別人懷疑你的能力時，用自信、帶點臭屁的語氣表示：「這種事怎麼可能難得倒我？」這裡的「拜託」不是請求。

Used when someone doubts your ability. It confidently—and often playfully—suggests that the task should be easy for you.

Examples

A：一個人開十六個小時的車，你真的可以嗎？

B：拜託，我誰啊！

A: Can you really drive for sixteen hours by yourself?

B: Come on—you know who I am!

A：你真的搶得到蔡依林演唱會的門票嗎？

B：拜託，我誰啊！我哪次沒買到？

A: Do you really think you can get tickets to Jolin Tsai’s concert?

B: Come on—you know who you’re talking to. When have I ever failed to get tickets?

03

（都）什麼年代了，還……

(dōu) shénme niándài le, hái… In this day and age, you still…?

「都什麼年代了，還有人恐同，還打同志啊。」

「什麼年代了，還給我摔同志啊？」

Usage

不是在問現在是哪一年，而是把某種觀念或行為視為早已過時，用來批評對方落伍或跟不上時代。

This is not a real question about the year. It criticizes an idea or behavior as outdated or backward.

Examples

都什麼年代了，還沒聽過行動支付？

Seriously? You’ve never heard of mobile payments?

什麼年代了，你還認為女人一定要結婚生子？

In this day and age, you still think women have to get married and have children?

都什麼年代了，還認為男生不能哭？

You still think men aren’t supposed to cry?

04

你把 X 當什麼啦？

Nǐ bǎ X dàng shénme la? What do you take X for?

「你把鬼都當什麼啦？你以為死人都互相認識的啊？」

「你又把同志當成什麼啦？你以為同志都互相認識的啊？」

Usage

「你把 X 當什麼啦？」用反問批評對方把某人或某個群體當成一種工具、角色或刻板形象。後面常再加一句問句或陳述句，把對方隱藏的想法直接說出來。

“What do you take X for?” is a rhetorical question used to criticize someone for treating a person or group as a tool, a fixed role, or a stereotype. It is often followed by a question or statement that exposes the assumption behind it.

Examples

我朋友每次有英文問題都來問我，覺得我是英國人就應該教他。把我當什麼啦？免費英文老師啊？

My friend comes to me whenever he has a question about English, as if being British means I’m supposed to teach him. What does he think I am—his free English tutor?

他每次喝酒都叫我半夜去接他，把我當什麼啦？以為我是 Uber 啊？

Every time he goes drinking, he expects me to pick him up in the middle of the night. What does he think I am—his personal Uber!?

A：中配的父母依親來台後，醫療費用讓台灣健保負擔不就好了？

B：你把台灣人當什麼啦？提款機啊？

A: Why not just have Taiwan’s health insurance cover their medical expenses after the parents of Chinese spouses move to Taiwan as dependents?

B: What do you think Taiwanese taxpayers are—an ATM?

05

V／SV 成這樣，你還想……喔？

V / SV chéng zhèyàng, nǐ hái xiǎng… ō? You’re already this…, and you still want to…?

「穿成這樣你還想進去喔？」

Usage

「V／SV 成這樣」表示動作或狀態已經到了一定程度，使對方後面想做的事情顯得不合理或不適合。可用來吐槽、阻止或警告對方。

“V/SV + chéng zhèyàng” shows that an action or condition has reached such a degree that what the other person wants to do next seems unreasonable or inappropriate. It can be used to tease, stop, or warn someone.

Examples

穿成這樣，你還想進去喔？

Dressed like that, you still think you can get in?

房間亂成這樣，你還想找得到東西喔？

Your room is such a mess—how do you expect to find anything?

都喝成這樣了，你還想自己開車喔？

You’re that drunk, and you still think you can drive?

06

叫你 V，你死不 V

jiào nǐ V, nǐ sǐ bù V I told you to V, but you just wouldn’t.

「為什麼叫你問路，你死不問路啊？」

Usage

「死不＋動詞」表示一個人非常固執，無論別人怎麼勸都不肯做。這個句型常用在事情出問題之後，責怪對方先前不聽勸。

“Sǐ bù + verb” means stubbornly refusing to do something despite being told or advised to do it. It often carries a strong, blaming tone.

Examples

叫你先備份，你死不備份，現在檔案不見了吧。

I told you to back up the files, but you just wouldn’t. Now they’re gone.

大家都叫他去看醫生，他死不去！

Everyone keeps telling him to see a doctor, but he flat-out refuses.

叫你早點出門，你死不聽，現在遲到了吧！

I told you to leave earlier, but you wouldn’t listen. Now we’re late.

語用重點不只在句型本身，也在說話者的情緒、關係與當下情境。
