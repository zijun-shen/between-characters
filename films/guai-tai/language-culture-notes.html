The Way We Speak
How Taiwanese people really speak.

渾身不對勁 hún shēn bú duì jìn

to feel uncomfortable / to feel that something is off
「我看到這些圖片就渾身不對勁。」

用法（Usage） 除了身體不舒服，也可以表示覺得怪怪的、不自在或不習慣。

Examples

我一天沒喝珍奶就渾身不對勁。

I feel off if I don't drink bubble tea for a day.

穿正式服裝上台演講，我覺得渾身不對勁。

I feel awkward giving a presentation in formal clothes.

老闆突然對我那麼好，我真是渾身不對勁。

My boss suddenly became so nice to me. It feels weird.

……好嗎？ hǎo ma

used to emphasize a point; not always a real question
「同路人也是有分等級的好嗎？」
「大家都大人了，你成熟一點好嗎？」

用法（Usage） 「……好嗎？」不一定是在問問題，也可以用來強調、抱怨、表達自己的立場，或提醒對方。

Examples

這很難的好嗎？

This is difficult, okay?

有什麼事情明天再說，我已經很累了好嗎？

Can we talk about it tomorrow? I'm already exhausted, okay?

我又不是故意的好嗎？

It's not like I did it on purpose, okay?

我們快來不及了，你快一點好嗎？

We're running out of time. Can you hurry up?

（你）認真？ rèn zhēn

Really? / Are you serious?
「我養你啊！」「你，認真？」

用法（Usage） 完整說法是「你是認重的嗎？」但台灣人常省略主詞，只說「認真？」或「你認真？」，通常用在感到驚訝、不敢相信，或懷疑對方是不是在開玩笑的時候。

Examples

A：欸，我們這週末去日本玩好不好？
B：蛤？認真？

Really? Are you serious?

A：我想跟老師說他的課很無聊。
B：你，認真？

Are you serious?

A：我決定明天辭職。
B：真的假的，你認真？

Seriously? Are you for real?

靠，還真的…… kào, hái zhēn de…

Wow! It actually…
「靠，還真的一樣。」

用法（Usage） 「靠」是台灣常見的語氣詞，用來表示驚訝、傻眼或不敢相信；「還真的……」表示原本不太相信，結果事情真的發生了。

Examples

A：外面好像下雨了。
B：靠！還真的下雨了耶！

Wow! It really is raining!

A：我聽說他考上台大了。
B：靠！還真的耶！

Wow! He actually got in!

搞什麼鬼啊！ gǎo shén me guǐ a

What the heck?!
「搞什麼鬼啊！連個強迫症都不會治療！」

用法（Usage） 表示驚訝、不滿或傻眼，不是真的和鬼有關，而是在說「到底怎麼回事？」

Examples

網站又壞掉了，搞什麼鬼啊！

What the heck? The website is down again!

他突然說不來了，搞什麼鬼啊！

What the heck? He suddenly said he wasn't coming!

也是啦 yě shì la

Fair enough. / I guess you're right.
「那是你上班上得還不夠久吧！？才第一天。」
「也是啦！」

用法（Usage） 表示被說服一點點，或不情願地承認對方說得有道理，通常帶一點無奈。

Examples

A：吃這間餐廳很貴耶！
B：可是它真的很好吃啊。
A：也是啦。

Fair enough.

A：今天不想上課。
B：可是期末考快到了。
A：也是啦。

I guess you're right.

Taiwanese Expressions
Expressions influenced by Taiwanese and everyday spoken Mandarin.

很秋

hěn qiūTaiwanesecool / cocky / impressive
🎬 「哇靠，當翻譯很秋耶！」

哇，你在 Google 工作喔？很秋耶！

Wow, you work at Google? That's pretty cool!

他考第一名以後就開始很秋。

After getting first place, he became a little cocky.

好康

hǎo kāngTaiwanesegood deal / benefit
🎬 「總覺得沒那麼好康。」

吃飯免費送 iPhone？這種好康的事有可能嗎？

Free iPhones just for eating here? Is that even possible?

聽說這家店今天買一送一，超好康！

I heard this store has a buy-one-get-one-free deal today. What a great bargain!

打槍

dǎ qiāngSlangto reject / to shoot down an idea
🎬 「被醫生打槍。」

我的提案被老闆打槍了。

My proposal was rejected by my boss.

他跟她告白，結果被打槍。

He confessed his feelings, but got rejected.

爛爆

làn bàoSlangterrible / awful
🎬 「這間爛爆。」

那部電影爛爆了。

That movie was terrible.

今天的趴踢真是爛爆了。

The party today was a total disaster.

Taiwanese Workplace Culture
Traditions, celebrations, and social customs in Taiwanese workplaces.
春酒 chūn jiǔ spring banquet農曆年後，公司或團體的開工聚餐。A company banquet held after Lunar New Year to celebrate the beginning of a new working year.
尾牙 wěi yá year-end party農曆年前，公司感謝員工的聚餐。A year-end company party held before Lunar New Year to thank employees.
年終 nián zhōng year-end bonus農曆年前常見的公司獎金。An annual bonus employees usually receive before Lunar New Year.
慶功 qìng gōng celebration dinner工作、活動或專案完成後的慶祝聚餐。A celebration meal held after finishing a project or achieving success.
續攤 xù tān after-party第一個聚會結束後，換地方繼續聚會。Going to another place to continue socializing after the first gathering ends.
Taiwanese Folk Beliefs
Traditional beliefs and rituals that remain part of everyday life in Taiwan.
陰陽水yīn yáng shuǐritual water 民間信仰中，混合冷熱水或用於儀式的水。 A mixture of hot and cold water used in Taiwanese folk beliefs for ritual cleansing or protection.
卡到陰kǎ dào yīnaffected by spirits 指被不好的靈體或陰氣影響，也常被拿來半開玩笑地說運氣很差。 A common Taiwanese expression meaning to be affected by bad spirits or negative energy.
放符fàng fúplacing a talisman 把符咒放進水裡、身上或特定位置，以求保護或化解不安。 A ritual practice of using talismans for protection or spiritual cleansing.
