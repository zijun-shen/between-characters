我們與世界之間

當代議題高階華語教材

從自我、他人到社會與世界，透過十六個當代議題練習思考、建立觀點，並用華語表達複雜而真實的想法。

Sixteen contemporary topics guide learners from the self and relationships to society, identity, and the wider world, while developing the ability to express complex ideas in Mandarin.
